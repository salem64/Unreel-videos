# What if all the air vanished for 5 seconds (sim3d, 2026-10-23)
claim | checked value | source
- sound needs a medium (air) to travel | yes, no air = silence | basic physics (NASA/any acoustics ref)
- sky is blue because air scatters sunlight | Rayleigh scattering; without air sky looks black, sun still bright | NASA/physics; astronauts see black sky
- body does not explode in vacuum | skin/tissue strong enough | aerospaceweb.org Q0291, WTAMU Science Questions
- useful consciousness in vacuum ~9-12 s (FAA AC 61-107), 10-15 s | so 5 s is survivable | aerospaceweb, WTAMU
- 1965 NASA JSC chamber suit leak: lost consciousness after ~14 s, last memory "saliva on tongue starting to boil", woke ~30 s later, no ill effects | aerospaceweb.org
- do not hold breath during rapid decompression: lung rupture risk | aerospaceweb (also standard aviation/diving advice)
- things in the air lose lift/drag support: planes/birds fall (free fall 5 s ~ 120 m) | physics; flames need oxygen -> go out
Simplifications: instant vanish of all air is hypothetical; air returning "slams back with a boom" (rushing air, shock); cartoon slow-motion for the 5 s HUD timer; no one is hurt on screen.

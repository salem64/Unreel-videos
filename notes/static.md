# Why doorknobs zap you (2026-10-20)
| claim | checked value | source |
|---|---|---|
| Air breakdown | ~3,000 V per mm (3 kV/mm), so 12,000 V jumps ~4 mm | Science Buddies "How far can sparks jump" |
| You feel a zap from ~2,000-3,000 V; typical zaps 3,000-5,000 V, range up to ~25,000 V | biologyinsights.com summary of ESD data (secondary) |
| Walking on vinyl: ~12,000 V at 20 % humidity vs ~250 V at 80 % | same summary (matches ESD Association table) |
| Discharge lasts a few hundred ns to ~1 microsecond, energy well under 1 millijoule | same summary |
Simplifications: 12,000 V is an example for a dry day (shown as "thousands"); finger gap mm shown is illustrative; electrons transfer by triboelectric charging (which side gains depends on materials).

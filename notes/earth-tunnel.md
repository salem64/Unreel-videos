# what if you fell through the earth – fact check
| claim | checked value | source |
|---|---|---|
| fall time ~38 min (realistic density) | 38 min 11 s (PREM), uniform Earth 42 min | Klotz 2015, arXiv 1308.1342; livescience.com/50312 |
| over 30,000 km/h | >8 km/s per source; own PREM-profile sim peak 9.9 km/s (35,750 km/h) shown on HUD | same + own sim (tools/sim3d) |
| gravity gets a bit stronger, then zero at center | max ~1.09 g near core-mantle boundary, then ~linear to 0 | Klotz 2015 |
| over 5,000 degrees, like the Sun's surface | inner core ~5,200–6,000 °C (ESRF 2013 ~6,000 °C); Sun surface ~5,500 °C | common refs |
| must grab the edge or fall back forever | without air you oscillate like a pendulum | livescience.com/50312 |
Simplifications: tunnel has no air and can't melt (stated in the video); gravity profile approximated from PREM points.
